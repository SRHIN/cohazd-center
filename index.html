About COHAZD

About the Center

The Center for One Health and Zoonotic Diseases is Africa’s forward command for predicting, preventing, and controlling diseases before they become disasters. We fuse science, surveillance, and digital innovation to stop outbreaks at their source , not after headlines break.

From zoonotic disease intelligence to digital health systems, we are building Africa’s frontline defense for a safer, healthier, more resilient future.

Programs & Initiatives

Explore some of our impactful and life-changing programs

Digital Surveillance

EpiWatch AI

EpiWatch AI transforms outbreak and historical disease data into clear, location-aware intelligence for faster public-health decisions across Africa.

Learn More north_east
Community Research

COMMUNACT

The COMMUNACT project is a collaborative research project aimed at understanding the factors influencing healthcare-seeking behavior for Mpox in Nigeria.

Learn More north_east
Zoonotic Defense

Canada-Africa MPox Project

We are leading Nigeria’s community research and advocacy component on Mpox (formerly ‘Monkeypox’) for the Canada-Africa Mpox project across several states in Nigeria.

Learn More north_east
Public Health

Youth 4 Covax

The Youth in COVAX project is a healthcare information awareness program for young people living in Oyo and Lagos State, aimed at training and educating youth on COVID-19 vaccines.
